# Project made by humans
AI is a tool to assist you, not to replace you.

## General rules
- DO NOT ask AI to scan the project for issues. Only report things you found yourself while using the app.
- DO NOT report things that are not a real use case or self-sabotage (e.g. explicitly typing weird stuff to break the app).
> _"Here are several small feature requests / improvements"_
- No, specially if you have just created your account. This is not how open-source works.
> _"I have 2 suggestions and found 2 bugs"_
- Please, open 4 separate issues, one for each task with detailed explanation, screenshots, drafts or how to reproduce the bug consistently.
> _"This is just your opinion"_
- Yes, this is my project. You can always fork it and do your own thing.

## Localization
- Only add or fix languages that you speak natively.
- DO NOT use translation apps or ask AI to do it.

## Pull requests
- Never commit code you didn't run on your machine and verified it works.
- If you don't review your code I will close your PR and most likely block you.
- DO NOT leave unnecessary comments around, good code should speak by itself.
  - If you want to keep a comment: 1 or 2 lines is enough, 2 paragraphs is unacceptable.
- DO NOT ask AI to automatically monitor the PR and fix issues.
- DO NOT use AI to reply to comments, write it yourself as a capable human being.
  - English is not my first language, I don't mind if you struggle, just do your best.
- DO NOT take feedback personal, I'm not being a jerk, I'm just maintaining the project.
- Avoid wasting time just to have your PR rejected. Open an Issue first to validate your idea
- If I don't accept your idea, that's ok, I don't hate you, it just doesn't fit.
